# Evidence checklist for an AI investment brief

This is a collection guide for a proposed assessment. It is not a statement that UniReverseAI has collected the listed evidence or delivered the illustrative project.

| Question | Evidence to collect | Decision consequence |
|---|---|---|
| What counts as one item? | Written process start/end, inclusion criteria, duplicate handling | Prevent different process boundaries being compared |
| Is the baseline representative? | Sample dates, number of items, mix, manual handling time, exclusions | Separate a measured baseline from an estimate |
| How much work is eligible? | Sample-level eligibility decisions with reasons | Bound the volume to which benefits can apply |
| Does the new workflow preserve quality? | Agreed tests for required fields, pricing rules, review points, exceptions | Treat quality as an acceptance gate rather than selling time savings alone |
| What handling work remains? | Observed per-item time including corrections and handoffs | Avoid counting an ideal demonstration as steady-state effort |
| Who does additional oversight? | Review/monitoring scope, time, staffing, actual incremental payment | Separate capacity use from additional cash cost |
| Will teams actually use it? | Observed eligible items routed through the changed workflow | Replace assumed adoption with observed adoption |
| Which expenses actually decrease? | Named cost line, owner, cash mechanism, timing, implementation cost | Support the cash realization rate or set it to zero |
| What is included in implementation? | Discovery, integrations, cleanup, training, rollout, contingency | Make one-time cost complete and incremental |
| What recurs after launch? | Usage assumptions, model/infra, support, reviews, exception workload | Prevent a low demo cost being presented as operating cost |
| What happens during rollout? | Ramp schedule, dual-running cost, training time, monthly volume | Decide whether the steady-state model is insufficient |
| Who owns the decision? | Business owner confirmation of assumptions and acceptance criteria | Give the assessment a real decision context |

## Suggested evidence register

Record each material assumption using these fields:

```text
Assumption ID:
Input name and unit:
Proposed value:
Status: estimated / measured / agreed / stale
Source or private evidence reference:
Sampling or measurement period:
Owner:
Known exclusions:
Next review trigger:
```

Keep customer records, pricing, contracts, and personal data in an agreed private location. A public example can show the measurement method and aggregate result only when its provenance and publication permission support that release.

## Stop or rescope signals

- The baseline includes work that the proposed change does not address.
- Additional correction or review effort removes the apparent time gain.
- The business cannot explain how released handling time changes cash spend.
- Quality or approval requirements are not met on representative cases.
- Ongoing costs or rollout timing are absent from the business case.
- Scenarios rely on one unmeasured assumption to produce all of the positive return.

These signals call for a smaller pilot, more evidence, a revised cost model, or a different workflow. They are useful outcomes of an opportunity assessment.
